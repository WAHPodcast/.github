## About WAHPod
The West African History Podcast (WAH-Pod) enters African voices, African archives, and African intellectual traditions while confronting colonial violence, gendered power, resistance, and historical memory. WAH-Pod brings academic scholarship into the public sphere through compelling storytelling grounded in primary sources, oral histories, and cutting-edge historical analysis.

### Background
WAH-Pod was started in 2026 by Dr. Nwando Achebe at Michigan State University. She was assisted by Henry Fagan and Emmanuel Ankomahene, both PhD candidates at MSU. The podcast is supported by the MSU African Studies Center, including initial production by Mike Green (formerly at the Center). Technical and production support was taken on by the Lab for the Education and Advancement in Digital Research.

The content of the podcast is focused on West Africa. The podcast website, [West African History Podcast] (http://westafricanhistorypod.com/index.html), includes supporting materials, including transcripts, background information, sources, and images. Further research is also available through the Journal of West African History.
